## C. Not answerable from law

- **5.3 in all five economies.** State shareholdings in digital firms, which ESCAP took from annual
  reports. We abstain by design.
- **AUS 3.4 and IND 3.4.** The top band needs a case where screening blocked an investment, which
  is a news fact, not a legal one.

## D. Our defects

- **Over-reads:** a provision stretched past what it says. AUS 10.1, 10.2, 11.3, 9.4, 12.5, 3.1;
  SGP 3.1 (law corporations), SGP 4.1 (the Arbitration Act's definition); THA 4.3 (Patent Act
  s.39); SGP 6.2 (a PSA transfer report read as a restriction).
- **Held but not surfaced or not recognised:**
  - AUS 5.2, a regression: the Telstra Corporation Act's 35% and 5% foreign-ownership caps are no
    longer found.
  - IND 8.1 and 8.2: scored "no framework" despite IT Act s.79 and the 2021 Intermediary Rules.
  - SGP 4.2 and IND 4.2: interim injunctions under the civil procedure codes missed.
  - IND 10.1 (read on the GM-organism rules) and IND 11.3 (maritime radio rules): wrong basis.
- **Binding documents we do not hold.** RBI directions, MCMC guidelines, NBTC notifications,
  Indian customs notifications. These have to be registered. They are also the case for the
  autonomous discovery the live test needs; see below.
- **ESCAP right on its own terms:** THA 11.2 and THA 12.5.
- **Reader failures:** 9 abstentions.

ESCAP's final-round output template settles what the sealed hour on 15 October asks:

- **The task:** one of nine economies (Thailand, Viet Nam, Indonesia, China, India, Kazakhstan,
  Lao PDR, Mongolia, the Russian Federation), **one pillar and two indicators**, announced at the
  start. "It may be a pillar you were never asked to work."
- **Engine A must fetch.** The Run Record sheet: "if no documents were fetched during the hour, C5a
  scores zero regardless of what the evidence file contains." Engine B must fetch zero.
- **Discovery is the tool's job:** "finds sources, downloads, reads and translates, extracts,
  maps", with "no manual steps"; anything typed by hand is recorded by the stewards.

So registering the missing documents fixes the submitted workbook, but it cannot be the answer on
the day: a hand-kept list does not exist for an economy we have not prepared, and a list consulted
from ESCAP's own citations would make every find KNOWN by construction. The tool has to find the
binding instruments below the Acts on its own, and do it inside the hour.
