## The one lever that is free: English-only word tests on our languages (15 cells)

test's word list is English and the provision is not. Sid's rules already carry the Thai equivalent

and MRA measure names); ours have none in Russian, Mongolian or Lao. The cell then abstains rather

## Our find

**LAO 6.2** (ours 0.5, ESCAP 0). The Agreement on the General Payment System requires a payment
system operator to "ເກັບຮັກສາຂໍ້ມູນທຸລະກໍາໄວ້ຢູ່ ສປປ ລາວ" (keep transaction data in the Lao PDR), in
Part 2 and again for cross-border operators in Part 3. That is a sector-specific local storage
requirement, the 0.5 band. ESCAP cites only the e-data and e-transactions laws.

## Contestable

*Added after the 28 September word-list fix:* **LAO 5.5** (ours 1, ESCAP 0). The Law on Radio
Frequency requires an applicant to have "ມີຖານະທາງດ້ານການເງິນທີ່ຫັ້ນດົງ" (a stable financial position)
to hold a licence; the band names minimum capital as a strict condition. Defensible either way.

- **MNG 5.7** (ESCAP 1). ESCAP scores no independent telecom authority; the Communications
  Regulatory Commission is established by the Law on Communications. Independence is a judgement.
- **MNG 6.3** (ours 0). ESCAP has two rows, 0 on the Personal Data Protection and Public Information
  laws and 1 on a ministerial order on processing sensitive data.
- **RUS 3.3** (ours 0, ESCAP 1). ESCAP's 1 rests on a 1997 Bank of Russia order with the note
  "Unable to locate an official source for this entry"; its other row, on 115-FZ, scores 0.
- **RUS 11.3** (ours 0.5, ESCAP 1). Our 0.5 rests on 126-FZ recognising test protocols obtained
  abroad "in accordance with international treaties"; ESCAP's own second row, on the EAEU Treaty,
  also scores 0.5.
- **LAO 7.1** (ours 0, ESCAP 0.5). The same law on both sides, the Electronic Data Protection Law;
  ESCAP reads it as sectoral (electronic data), we read it as horizontal.

## Not answerable from legislation

**5.3** in Mongolia and Lao (government shares in telecom operators), as in all five of Sid's.

## Outside the corpus's scope: Eurasian Economic Union law (3)

**RUS 1.4, 10.2, 12.5.** ESCAP answers each from a decision of the Eurasian Economic Commission (an
anti-dumping duty, the non-tariff measures list, the personal-use goods threshold). EAEU law applies
in Russia directly and is not published on a Russian federal portal, which is a scope limit the
profile already states.

## We are wrong (53)

**The law was never registered or never retrieved (the answering instrument is not in what was
read).**
- MNG 2.1, 2.3 (the procurement law was read, its preference clauses not reached), 3.5, 6.4
  (Personal Data Protection Law Art. 14), 8.3 and 9.4 (the Digital Content Service conditions and
  the Law on Permits), 9.3 (Law on Advertisement), 12.4.5 (the Bank of Mongolia payment procedures),
  12.5.
- RUS 2.3 (Government resolutions 1875, 1442, 486), 4.9 (374-FZ, not registered), 6.3 (152-FZ
  Art. 18(5)), 8.1 (Civil Code Art. 1253.1 not among the framework candidates), 10.4 (Government
  Resolution 311), 7.2 (187-FZ is now registered; pillar 7 was not re-read for Russia).
- LAO 2.3, 6.4, 8.2, 8.3 (Decree 327), 8.4 (Decree 164), 10.1, 12.01 and 12.3 (Decree on
  E-Commerce 296), 12.5, 9.3 (Decision 1061).

**Read, and the measure missed or set aside** (mostly the confirmation pass ruling the finding out):
- MNG 4.01, 7.1 (the pillar 7 re-read no longer showed the framework rule it showed on 27 Sep).
- RUS 2.1 (44-FZ national regime), 3.1 (Mass Media Law's foreign ownership cap, Banks Law),
  4.01 (Civil Code Part IV patent attorney), 6.4 (152-FZ Art. 12), 8.3 (149-FZ Art. 10.1

  system operators), 4.5 (the Civil Code Part IV copyright provisions were held as repealed).
- LAO 4.01, 5.1, 10.2 (one compliance measure found where ESCAP counts several), 12.4.1, 12.4.2.

**Over-claims (a provision read as a measure it is not).**
- MNG 3.4 (nine "screening mechanisms", counting procedural clauses of one Investment Law),
  6.2 (government databases, not private data -- the `appliesOnlyToGovernmentData` flag),
  7.4 (a cyber-security procedure's data custodian list read as a DPO), 7.5 (state bodies' own
  processing, not access to data held by others).
- LAO 4.1 (confidentiality clauses in the anti-dumping and competition laws read as full trade
  secret protection; ESCAP's 0.5 is the band for clauses in other laws), 7.5, 11.3 (standards
  duties in the radio-frequency and surveying laws), 12.4.7 ("use payment instruments in
  accordance with terms and conditions" is not a restriction).
- MNG/LAO 5.1, 5.4 and LAO 5.4 (held: the provision names no party it binds, in drafting that
  states the duty passively).
